# IPHONE TUTORIAL

1. Install **Google Chrome** on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Open Chrome and go to **Discord**.
3. Log in to Discord through the **browser** (do not use the Discord app).
4. Once logged in, copy the **script for the `iphone.js` file**.
5. Paste it into Chrome’s **search bar** and press **Enter**.
6. Your **token** will be automatically copied to the clipboard.

# PC TUTORIAL
1. Open **Discord** in your browser.
2. Press **Ctrl + Shift + I** (Windows) or **Cmd + Option + I** (Mac) to open the console.
3. Paste the **provided script** in `pc.js` into the console and submit it.
4. Your **token** will be automatically copied to the clipboard.
